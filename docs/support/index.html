About This Release

Chess Grandmaster version 1.0 is an offline chess practice and study app. You can play against built-in chess-engine logic or watch chess engines play automatically.

How to Start a Game

  1. Open Chess Grandmaster.
  2. Tap Menu.
  3. Open Launch Pad.
  4. Choose Play with Chess Engine or Watch and Learn from Chess Engines.
  5. Select AI Strength and available options.
  6. Tap Start game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. Version 1.0 supports offline play on this device.

Do I need an account?

No. Accounts are not required for this release.

Does this release include live online play?

No. Version 1.0 does not enable live play, chat, player queues, accounts, or player-to-player challenges.

Why does stronger AI take longer?

Stronger AI levels calculate deeper tactical lines before moving.

Where do I change board themes or pieces?

Open Menu, then Settings. Theme, piece style, avatars, music, sounds, and timers can be adjusted there.

Does the app show ads?

No. Version 1.0 does not include advertising.
