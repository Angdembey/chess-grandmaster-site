About This Release

Chess Grandmaster AI version 1.1 is an offline chess practice and study app. You can play against built-in chess-engine logic or use Watch and Learn to observe chess engines play automatically.

Premium Unlock

Beginner and Club AI levels are available without purchase. Premium Unlock is an optional, one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks Tournament, Master, Grandmaster, and Maximum AI levels in Play with Chess Engine and Watch and Learn. It is not a subscription.

How do I purchase Premium Unlock?

  1. Open Launch Pad.
  2. Select Tournament, Master, Grandmaster, or Maximum.
  3. Choose Unlock Premium and complete the purchase through the App Store or Google Play.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

  1. Open the Premium Unlock screen.
  2. Select Restore or Restore Purchases.
  3. Use the same store account that originally purchased Premium Unlock.

Restoring is useful after reinstalling the app or moving to another supported device. Premium Unlock is associated with the store account and platform where it was purchased; Apple and Google purchases do not transfer between stores.

What if my purchase or restoration does not complete?

Confirm that the device is online, that you are signed in with the original store account, and that purchases are allowed on the device. Restart the app and try Restore Purchases again. If the problem continues, contact support and include the device type and a description of what happened. Do not send payment-card details or account passwords.

How to Start a Game

  1. Open Chess Grandmaster.
  2. Tap Menu.
  3. Open Launch Pad.
  4. Choose Play with Chess Engine or Watch and Learn from Chess Engines.
  5. Select AI Strength and available options.
  6. Tap Start game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. Version 1.1 supports offline play on this device. An internet connection is needed when purchasing or restoring Premium Unlock through the applicable app store.

Do I need an account?

No. Accounts are not required for this release.

Does this release include live online play?

No. Version 1.1 does not enable live play, chat, player queues, accounts, or player-to-player challenges.

Why does stronger AI take longer?

Stronger AI levels calculate deeper tactical lines before moving.

Where do I change board themes or pieces?

Open Menu, then Settings. Theme, piece style, avatars, music, sounds, and timers can be adjusted there.

Does the app show ads?

No. Version 1.1 does not include advertising.
