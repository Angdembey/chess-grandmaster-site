About Chess Grandmaster

Chess Grandmaster is designed for chess practice and study on phone and tablet. Version 1.0 focuses on offline play with built-in chess-engine logic.

Version 1.0 Scope

This release does not include analytics, ads, crash-reporting SDKs, backend calls, active accounts, sign-in, chat, live player queues, or personal data collection.
