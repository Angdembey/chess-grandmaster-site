App Provider

Chess Grandmaster is provided by Dhana Angdembey.

Summary for Version 1.0

Chess Grandmaster version 1.0 is designed for offline chess practice and study on this device. No account is required for this release.

  • No analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No crash-reporting SDK.
  • No backend calls.
  • No active accounts or sign-in.
  • No chat or live player queue.
  • No personal data collected.

Information We Collect

Chess Grandmaster version 1.0 does not collect personal information.

The app may store preferences locally on your device, such as board theme, piece style, avatar selection, sound settings, timer settings, and gameplay preferences. These settings remain on your device for app functionality.

Online Features

Version 1.0 does not enable online player accounts, sign-in, live play, chat, player queues, or player-to-player challenges.

Advertising and Tracking

Chess Grandmaster version 1.0 does not display advertising and does not track users across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

Chess Grandmaster version 1.0 does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

Chess Grandmaster version 1.0 does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
