App Provider

Chess Grandmaster is provided by Dhana Angdembey.

Summary for Version 1.1

Chess Grandmaster AI version 1.1 is designed for offline chess practice and study on this device. No account is required for this release. The optional Premium Unlock is processed by the App Store or Google Play.

  • No analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No crash-reporting SDK.
  • No developer-operated backend calls.
  • No active accounts or sign-in.
  • No chat or live player queue.
  • No personal data collected.

Information We Collect

Chess Grandmaster AI version 1.1 does not collect personal information.

The app may store preferences locally on your device, such as board theme, piece style, avatar selection, sound settings, timer settings, gameplay preferences, and Premium entitlement state. These settings remain on your device for app functionality.

In-App Purchases

Version 1.1 offers an optional, one-time Premium Unlock. Purchases and restorations are processed by Apple through StoreKit on iOS and iPadOS, or by Google through Google Play Billing on Android. The app provider does not receive or store your payment-card number or store-account password.

When you purchase or restore Premium Unlock, the app communicates with the applicable app store to obtain product and entitlement information. Apple or Google may process purchase, account, device, and transaction information under their own privacy policies. The app may store the resulting Premium entitlement locally so unlocked features remain available offline.

Premium entitlement does not transfer between Apple and Google store accounts. Restore Purchases requires an internet connection and the same applicable store account that originally made the purchase.

Online Features

Version 1.1 does not enable online player accounts, sign-in, live play, chat, player queues, or player-to-player challenges. Store connectivity is used only when requesting product, purchase, or restoration information from the applicable app store.

Advertising and Tracking

Chess Grandmaster AI version 1.1 does not display advertising and does not track users across apps or websites.

Data Sharing

Chess Grandmaster AI version 1.1 does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Store transactions are processed by Apple or Google as described above.

Children's Privacy

Chess Grandmaster AI version 1.1 does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
